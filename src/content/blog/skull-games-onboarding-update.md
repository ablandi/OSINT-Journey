Back in August I wrote about [getting the onboarding email](/blog/skull-games-onboarding) from Skull Games. Here's where the process stands now.

After the assessment came the membership paperwork and fee, and then the background investigation forms, which I signed and returned in mid-September. On September 24, the email I'd been waiting on arrived: the background investigation had been processed.

What's next is the part I don't control the timing of — the Slack and SharePoint invites, which I'm expecting in early October. Until those land and onboarding is actually complete, I'm not calling myself a volunteer. That's a line I want to keep clean.

One thing the process clarified for me: Skull Games asks that you not volunteer with another organization doing the same work, so I stepped back from the Guardian Group exam pipeline I'd been working through. One mission, one team — it was the right call.

I'll keep posting updates as this moves.
