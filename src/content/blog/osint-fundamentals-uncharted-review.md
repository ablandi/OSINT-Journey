Back in May of 2024, I took **OSINT Fundamentals**, a two-day live course offered by [Uncharted](https://www.unchartered.info/) — a digital investigations and training firm — and led by Justen Charters himself. It was one of the earlier structured learning experiences in my OSINT journey, and looking back on it now, it did a lot to shape how I approach research.

## The format: live over Zoom

This wasn’t a pre-recorded video course you watch on your own time. It was two full days, live over Zoom, with Justen teaching in real time. That format mattered more than I expected going in. Being able to ask questions as they came up, watch an experienced investigator work through examples live, and hear his reasoning out loud — not just the polished final answer, but the actual process of chasing leads and adjusting mid-investigation — is something recorded courses rarely capture.

There’s also something to be said for the pace of a live cohort. Knowing other students were working through the same material in real time kept the energy up across both days in a way self-paced learning just doesn’t.

## What the course covered

As the name suggests, OSINT Fundamentals built from the ground up: what open source intelligence actually is and isn’t, how to think like an investigator rather than a casual searcher, and the foundational techniques for finding and verifying information from public sources. Justen’s background shows in the teaching — Uncharted works across digital investigations and due diligence for real clients, and the examples and framing in the course reflect people who do this work professionally, not just teach it theoretically.

What I appreciated most was the emphasis on *process over tools*. Tools come and go, and half the specific platforms or services mentioned in any OSINT course will be deprecated or paywalled within a couple of years. The fundamentals of how to approach a question, where to look first, how to pivot when a lead dies, and how to verify what you find — those don’t expire, and that’s what the course drilled in.

## How it fits into my journey

This course came at the right time for me. I already had the motivation — that part had been lit years earlier — but I was still piecing together technique from scattered articles, videos, and practice. OSINT Fundamentals gave me a structured baseline to build on, and it set me up well for everything that came after, including eventually sitting for the [PORP exam](/blog/porp-exam-review).

If you’re early in your OSINT journey and want something more interactive than a Udemy playlist but more accessible than a full certification track, a live fundamentals course like this is a solid investment. Learning directly from someone who does this work for a living, in real time, is worth a lot more than the cost of admission.
