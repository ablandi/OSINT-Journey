In [my last update](/blog/skull-games-onboarding-update) I drew a line for myself: I wasn't going to call myself a volunteer until the invites landed and onboarding was actually underway. That line mattered to me — claiming something before it's real is a fast way to cheapen it.

Yesterday afternoon, the invites landed. The Slack invite from the Skull Games Task Force arrived, along with the account setup emails.

I'm officially onboarding as a volunteer OSINT analyst.

What's ahead: training materials, a case study, and a practice case before I'm fully in the rotation. I'm not operational yet, and I won't pretend otherwise. But I'm in the door, in the community, and doing the work to get ready.

Back in August, getting that first email felt like the door cracking open. Now it's open. Time to walk through it.
