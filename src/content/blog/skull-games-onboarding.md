Last month I submitted an application to volunteer my skills with [Skull Games](https://www.skullgames.org/), the nonprofit that puts OSINT to work hunting down traffickers.

A couple of days ago they emailed me about onboarding.

I was grinning from ear to ear. I felt like a middle school girl who just found out her crush likes her back. I was doing cartwheels — internally, for the record.

If you have read [anything else here](/blog/am-i-ready-guardian-group), you know why this one hit the way it did. Skull Games and the people behind it were on the short list of examples I was looking up to back when my chest was poking out and I thought one training had made me ready. Getting an email from them was not a badge. It was the door I had been [training toward](/blog/porp-exam-review) actually opening a crack.

There was an assessment. I submitted it today.

Now I wait to see how I did.

So I will keep you all posted. Mostly I just wanted to share the excitement.
